Amazon Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

This policy describes how Brutal Game Studios handles information related to products we sell on Amazon and apps and games we distribute through the Amazon Appstore. See the privacy notice for your specific app for details about its features, permissions, and data practices.

Amazon orders and payments

Amazon handles checkout and payment for purchases made through Amazon. Depending on the product and who fulfills the order, Amazon may make limited order information available to us. This can include the item ordered, order number, delivery information needed for seller fulfillment, and messages sent through Amazon. We use this information to fulfill orders, respond to support requests, handle returns or refunds, and meet applicable recordkeeping requirements. If we arrange delivery, we share the information needed for delivery with the carrier or fulfillment provider.

Amazon handles its own account, checkout, and payment data under its Privacy Notice.

Apps and games

Depending on the app, settings, preferences, and game progress may be stored on your device. If an app uses Amazon services to verify a license, confirm a purchase, or restore access, the app may use purchase status, an entitlement result, or a store identifier for that purpose. When an app uses online features, advertising, diagnostics, or other third-party services, the app-specific notice describes the information involved, why it is used, and who receives it.

Support and other disclosures

If you contact us, we receive your message, the contact information you provide, and any attachments or order details you choose to include. We use that information to respond, investigate issues, and follow up as needed. We may share relevant information with service providers who help us deliver support, and we may disclose it when required by law.

Retention and your choices

We keep order and support information for as long as needed to handle the order or request, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. You can manage app permissions in your device settings. Where the device offers a clear-data option, you can use it to remove information stored locally by an app. Clearing app data may remove saved progress and settings.

You can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold. We may verify your request and retain records where required by law. Use Amazon's privacy controls for information held by Amazon.

Children's privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child shared personal information with us, please contact us so we can review your request and take appropriate action. The privacy notice for the specific app describes any child-directed features and related data practices.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email Brutal Game Studios at brutalgamestudios1@gmail.com. For an order issue, you can also contact us through your Amazon order.
