Board Apps Privacy

This policy covers Garage Board, Kitchen Board, Home Board, and Kids Board for Vega OS and Fire OS devices.

Your board

Jobs, checklist entries, notes, quantities, and appearance choices are stored on your device. The apps do not require a Brutal Game Studios account or send board contents to Brutal Game Studios. You can delete individual entries or jobs in the app and remove all app data through your device settings.

Remote dictation

Where available, dictation is a feature of the Fire TV keyboard. The app receives the resulting text as it would receive typed text. The apps do not record or receive your microphone audio. Amazon manages its keyboard and voice features under its own privacy terms.

Trial and purchases

On Vega OS, the app stores the 30-day trial dates on the device. Amazon Appstore handles payment and purchase restoration. The app uses an Appstore account identifier and purchase status to check ownership and stores a short-lived verification record on the device. Brutal Game Studios receives neither that local record nor your payment details. Kids Board asks for a grown-up before opening purchase controls.

Other Appstore listings

If you open Check out our other apps, the app requests a public list of studio apps hosted on GitHub Pages. The request does not include your board, an account identifier, or a device identifier. As with an ordinary website request, the host receives connection information such as your IP address. Selecting a listing opens Amazon Appstore, which operates under Amazon's privacy terms.

Kids Board keeps its routine screens offline. A grown-up step comes before the app fetches or opens the Appstore listings. The apps do not include ad or analytics SDKs.
