Free promotion plan for BGS2026 Finds

The first experiment is three original short videos and two relevant, permitted guide posts over a week. Focus on maker and retro-gaming decisions, then use actual questions and report data to choose the next topic. The targets describe work to do, not promised views or commissions.

Select a useful guide destination

Use a specific article when it answers the question in your post. Use the Finds library link in a general profile. These are live destinations:

  • Maker checklist
  • Retro gift checklist
  • Streaming checklist
  • Guide library

Make the first video

Topic: A 110-piece kit does not mean 110 different tools.

Suggested 25–40 second structure:

  1. Open with the question in large readable text.
  2. Explain that a kit can contain repeated consumables and different accessory types.
  3. Show your own three-line checklist: tool model, material, next task.
  4. End with: “Our maker buying guide has the full checklist. Find BGS2026 Finds through the profile link.”

Use your own voice, footage, typography or a screen recording of our guide. Do not present it as a hands-on test of the Dremel kit. Show the affiliate relationship clearly in the promotion where relevant.

For the second video, ask whether a miniature arcade is a display collectible or something the recipient will play for long sessions. Show game list, screen/control size and power as the checks. For the third, explain how to check a TV's existing apps before buying a streaming stick.

Publish with a clear route

Put the live library in a profile link you control. On YouTube, channel-profile links are clickable; Shorts description/comment URLs are not. Long-form external-link features have their own eligibility. Check YouTube's current link guidance before choosing the call to action.

The ready post drafts include disclosures. Adapt them to what your video actually shows. Avoid broad claims about savings, sales volume or personal testing that the guide does not establish.

Participate in communities

Read the specific community rules and permitted promotion threads. Answer an actual question fully enough that the answer is useful on its own. If a guide link is relevant and permitted, disclose that you operate the site. Do not paste the same pitch into unrelated conversations. See Reddit's spam guidance.

No community has been selected or contacted on your behalf by this package. Posting in a community with a relevant subject does not imply its moderators allow promotion.

Keep measurement simple

Use the weekly log to connect a topic, published destination and report date range. Compare the data available from each system separately: video views, profile-link clicks, Google search clicks and Amazon activity measure different stages. The current site has no added visitor analytics dashboard.

If a topic earns questions, improve the guide to answer them. If people see a video but cannot find the guide, make the destination clearer. If a product is a poor fit for the audience, revise the selection before adding hundreds of similar entries.

What to avoid spending on

This plan requires no ads, bought followers, paid SEO plugins, subscription video tools or inventory. Use the tools and accounts you already control. Free plans can still have optional paid upgrades; do not approve an upgrade to complete these steps.

Revenue is uncertain. Do not budget for an expected first-sale date or treat screenshots of another seller's revenue as evidence of your likely profit.
