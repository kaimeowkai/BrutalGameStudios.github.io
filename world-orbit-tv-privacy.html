World Orbit TV Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

World Orbit TV is developed under the BrutalGameStudios publisher brand. This policy describes the app’s local settings, Amazon purchase integration, and connections to broadcast and guide services.

Settings stored on your device

The app stores preferences, appearance settings, favorites, the last watched channel identifier, audio settings, optional guide source URLs and cached programme information in its private device storage. The app does not provide its own account system or a developer-operated cloud synchronization service.

Amazon purchases

Amazon handles in-app purchases and payments. World Orbit TV receives an app-specific Amazon account identifier, marketplace information and purchase receipts/status so it can unlock and restore full access. It may keep a local purchase cache; cached ownership is usable for up to 30 days after a successful check for the same Amazon account and product. The cache can remain on the device until it is updated or local app data is removed. The app does not receive your payment card details. Amazon’s own terms and privacy policies apply to its account, Appstore and payment services.

Streams, schedules and other third-party requests

The app connects directly to providers of channel streams, programme schedules, channel information and, where available, captions. Optional guide URLs supplied by you are contacted to download their guide data. Network providers receive the technical information needed to deliver these resources, which may include your IP address, the resource requested and request headers. The requested resource may identify the channel, schedule or caption being accessed. These independent providers may retain technical, viewing or advertising records under their own policies; their retention periods are not controlled by this app. Broadcasters may serve advertising and apply their own privacy practices, availability rules and regional restrictions. World Orbit TV does not include a separate advertising or analytics SDK.

Data we do not request

The app does not request your contacts, microphone recordings, photos, precise device location or payment card details. Searching channels and selecting favorites do not create a developer-operated cloud profile. Broadcast and platform services may maintain their own technical or transaction records.

Your choices and deletion

You can change preferences and favorites in the app. Removing a guide source stops future guide requests to that source. You can remove the app’s local settings and caches through your device’s app-data controls or by uninstalling the app; this may remove favorites and saved preferences. Removing local data does not delete Amazon purchase records or records held by independent service providers. Contact those providers for information about their records.

Support

If you choose to email support, BrutalGameStudios receives the information you include and uses it to respond. Support mail is handled through Gmail, which also processes that correspondence under its own service policies. You may use the support address to request deletion of support correspondence held by the publisher; requests affecting Amazon or broadcaster records must be directed to those providers. Do not send Amazon passwords, payment card numbers or other unnecessary sensitive information. Contact brutalgamestudios1@gmail.com with privacy or support questions.

Children and content

World Orbit TV is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and older. It is a general live-channel guide and player, and is not designed specifically for children. This intended audience is not a guarantee that every live programme is suitable for every teenager. Broadcast content and advertising depend on the selected provider. Device and Amazon parental controls may apply; the app does not currently provide verified programme-level age filtering.

Changes

The publisher will update the published policy when the app’s data practices change. The effective date of this policy appears above.
