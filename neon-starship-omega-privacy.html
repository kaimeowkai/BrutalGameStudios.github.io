Neon Starship Omega

Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026 · Version 2.1.0

This policy covers the Amazon Fire tablet release of Neon Starship Omega, published by Brutal Game Studios. Contact: brutalgamestudios1@gmail.com.

Gameplay and information on your device

The game stores settings, saved runs, local high scores, ship selections, ship unlocks and earned salvage in private app storage on your device. These game records are not uploaded to an online leaderboard or sent to the developer. The game has no chat, multiplayer service, advertising, analytics or automatic crash-reporting service.

Amazon purchases and license verification

The Amazon edition is a one-time paid download. It does not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions. Amazon handles the purchase, payment and Amazon account. The developer does not receive your payment-card details or Amazon password.

At launch, the Amazon Appstore SDK checks your license through the Amazon Appstore service on your device. Amazon processes the account entitlement, installation and device information needed to authenticate the app and verify the license. The game receives a license status and request identifier and uses them to allow access or show a recovery message. It does not use licensing information for advertising or user profiling, or transmit your gameplay records to the developer. Amazon handles its services under the Amazon Privacy Notice.

Gameplay runs locally. Internet access may be needed to obtain or renew your Amazon license. Offline play depends on a valid license cached by Amazon Appstore.

Permissions and device features

The game uses touch input, optional gamepad input and audio playback. It does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, location or personal media library. The Amazon SDK communicates with Amazon Appstore for licensing. The game does not operate a developer account system or developer server.

Retention and deletion

Local game records remain until replaced by gameplay, cleared through your device's app-storage settings, or removed when you uninstall. Clearing storage deletes settings, saved runs and local scores. Operating-system backup or device-transfer features may copy and restore app data according to your device settings; manage those copies through your device or backup provider. Manage Amazon account, purchase and licensing records through Amazon.

Support and children

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request and keep it only as needed for support and applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by contacting the address above. Please do not send passwords, payment details or children's personal information.

The game does not ask for age, contact details or an online profile, and does not provide advertising or social features. Parents can clear local game data using device settings.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app's practices change. The current policy and effective date will be available at this address.
