Privacy Policy - paid Amazon edition

Effective November 5, 2026

This policy covers the paid Amazon Fire tablet edition of Offcut Pilot, package com.brutalgamestudios.cutcalc.paid, published by Brutal Game Studios. Contact: brutalgamestudios1@gmail.com.

Your workshop projects

Project names, material dimensions, stock costs, quantities, kerf and trim settings, part lists, molding angles, saved projects and the current draft are stored in private app storage on your device. Calculations run locally. The app does not upload these projects to the developer or operate a cloud project service.

You can export a PDF or SVG through Android's document picker. You choose the file name and destination. If you select a cloud-storage provider, that provider handles the exported file under its own terms and privacy policy. Exporting is your explicit action; Exports are not automatically emailed or uploaded by Offcut Pilot.

Amazon purchase and license verification

The paid edition is a one-time purchase, with no subscriptions, in-app purchases or advertising. Amazon handles purchases, payment details and the Amazon account. The developer does not receive your Amazon password or payment-card details.

The Amazon Appstore SDK checks the app's license through Amazon Appstore on your device. Amazon processes the device, app-installation and account-entitlement information needed to authenticate the installation and verify access. The app receives a license status and request identifier, used for app functionality, fraud prevention and license recovery. Workshop project contents are not attached to license requests by Offcut Pilot. This licensing integration is not used by us for advertising, behavioral profiling or marketing.

Amazon manages its services under the Amazon Privacy Notice. The app relies on Amazon's valid cached license for offline access. An internet connection may be required to obtain or renew that license.

Permissions and other services

The paid app does not include an advertising SDK, analytics SDK, social features or automatic developer crash-reporting service. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or general media library. Access to an exported document is limited to the destination you select through the document picker. There is no Offcut Pilot login or developer-hosted account.

Delete your information

To remove a saved project, open Saved and choose Delete for that project. To clear the current draft, start a new project. To remove all private app data, use your device's app-storage settings or uninstall the app. Automatic Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for app data. Exported PDF/SVG documents are separate files and remain until you delete them in your chosen storage provider.

We cannot remotely access or delete projects kept only on your device. To request deletion of information you have emailed to support, email brutalgamestudios1@gmail.com with the subject Offcut Pilot data deletion. Manage Amazon purchase, account and licensing records through Amazon.

Support and audience

If you contact support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request and retain it only as needed for support and applicable obligations. Do not send passwords, payment information or sensitive personal information. This is a workshop planning utility intended for adult DIY users and workshops, not a child-directed service.

Separate editions and changes

This policy applies to the paid edition. Any future advertising-supported free edition will have a separate, updated disclosure describing its actual ad provider and data practices before publication. We may update this policy when the app's practices change; the effective date will appear here.
