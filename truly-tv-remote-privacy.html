Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026
App package: com.brutalgamestudios.trulyuniversalsmarttvremote

Privacy and support contact: brutalgamestudios1@gmail.com

Truly TV Remote, developed by Brutal Game Studios, connects your phone to compatible TVs and streaming devices. The app does not require an account and includes no advertising, analytics, or automatic crash-reporting service. The developer does not operate a server that receives your remote-control activity.

Local network and pairing

The app discovers and communicates with devices on your network. Device names, network addresses, identifiers, pairing tokens, and command preferences are stored in the app's private storage to reconnect and remember your choices. Commands, typed text, app-launch requests, and voice requests are sent to the device you select. Some TV protocols use unencrypted local network connections; use the remote on a network you trust.

Voice and microphone

Microphone access is optional and requested for voice features. Fire TV and Google TV voice modes send microphone audio to your selected TV or streaming device; its assistant provider may process the request through its cloud service. Dictation and keyword modes use the Android speech-recognition service selected on your phone. That service may send audio to its provider for recognition. The app receives recognized text to perform the requested action.

The app does not save audio recordings or upload them to the developer. TV, assistant, and speech-recognition providers handle requests under their own privacy policies. You can deny or revoke microphone permission in Android settings and continue using other remote controls.

Backgrounds and preferences

Only the photo or video you choose is imported into the app's private storage for your background. The app does not upload your background to the developer or require access to your whole media library. Appearance, sounds, tutorial state, and saved device preferences are stored on your phone.

External services and support

Wallpaper and search links open external websites in your browser. Their privacy policies apply when you visit them. If you email support, the developer receives the email address and information you choose to send and uses it to answer your request. Do not include pairing codes or passwords.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains until you clear the app's storage or uninstall. Android backup and device-transfer features may copy or restore app data according to your device settings. Manage those copies through your Android or backup-provider settings. Revoke saved remote authorization in your TV settings if needed.

Support emails are kept only as needed to handle your inquiry and any applicable legal obligations. Contact brutalgamestudios1@gmail.com to request deletion of support correspondence.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app behavior changes. The latest published version and update date are available on this page. Privacy-policy text is also available within the app under Appearance > Settings.
