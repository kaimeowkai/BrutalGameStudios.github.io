BRUTAL GAME STUDIOS

Ready, Set, Go

Privacy Policy — paid Amazon Fire TV edition

Effective November 5, 2026

This policy covers Ready, Set, Go, package com.brutalgamestudios.readysetgo.paid, by Brutal Game Studios. It is a child-directed family routine app with parent controls. Contact: brutalgamestudios1@gmail.com.

Your family's routine stays on the device

Task names, typed instructions, durations, progress, sound preferences, optional alarm settings and a salted hash of the parent PIN are stored in the app's private device storage. The developer does not operate a cloud account, routine synchronization, advertising, analytics, behavioral profiling, social features or automatic crash-reporting service. No separate app account, subscription or in-app purchase is required. The app does not request location, contacts or camera access.

Optional parent messages and device voice

A parent may choose to record a short instruction if the device has an accessible microphone. Microphone permission is requested only for this parent-initiated feature. Recording stops when the parent stops it, after 30 seconds, or when the app is left. There is no background listening or wake-word detection. Voice recordings are not used to identify people, infer personal traits or train AI models.

Parents may instead select an audio file through a compatible system document picker. Only the selected file is read and copied into the app's private storage; the app does not request broad access to the media library. Playback must be supported and the imported clip must be no more than 45 seconds and 8 MB. An optional installed English offline text-to-speech voice can read task instructions. The app rejects network-required voices. Parent recordings take priority. Recording, importing and offline voice availability vary by device; the Alexa remote microphone is not assumed available to this app.

The app does not upload recordings, task instructions or PINs to the developer or attach them to Amazon license requests. A system document provider independently handles any file you choose from that provider under its own privacy policy. Avoid putting sensitive information in task names or messages shown on a shared TV.

Optional morning music alarm

Parents can choose an alarm time, repeat days and local music level. These settings remain on the device. Android's alarm service handles scheduling; alarm and boot permissions allow an enabled schedule to run and be restored after restart. The alarm is off by default, has no cloud schedule and does not contact another TV or smart-home device. Standby waking depends on the TV. The included instrumental is bundled with the app. Alarm music stops after two minutes or when you leave its screen. No room audio is recorded by this feature.

Amazon purchase and license verification

This is a one-time paid, ad-free edition. Amazon handles purchases, payment details and the Amazon account. The developer does not receive your Amazon password or payment-card details. The Amazon Appstore SDK checks the license through Amazon Appstore on your device. Amazon processes the app-installation, device and account-entitlement information needed to authenticate the installation. The app receives a license status and request identifier for purchase verification and fraud prevention, not advertising or marketing. Routine data and recordings are not included in license requests by us.

Amazon provides its store services under the Amazon Privacy Notice. The app relies on Amazon's valid cached license for offline access; an internet connection may be needed to obtain or renew it. We do not add advertising identifiers or a third-party advertising SDK.

Children and parent controls

The everyday routine is designed for children and families. Setup, recording, importing and alarm configuration are behind a local parent PIN. This PIN is a convenience barrier, not verification of identity, age or legally verified parental consent. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information on developer servers, sell it, share it for targeted advertising or solicit children to contact support. Parents should handle purchases, permissions and support requests.

Retention and deletion

Parents can remove a recording in its task editor or replace it with a new one, edit task information and switch the alarm off in parent controls. To delete all private app data, use the device's app-storage settings or uninstall the app. Automatic Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for app data. Original audio files selected from outside the app remain in their source location until you delete them separately. We cannot remotely access or delete information kept only on your device.

If a parent contacts support, we receive their email address and what they choose to send. We use it to answer the request and retain it only as needed for support and applicable obligations. Do not send passwords, payment details, children's recordings or other sensitive information. For deletion of support correspondence, email brutalgamestudios1@gmail.com. Manage Amazon account, purchase and license records through Amazon.

Changes

We may update this policy when app practices change. The effective date appears above.
